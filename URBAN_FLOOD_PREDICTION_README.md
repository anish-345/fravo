## 📌 Overview

Urban flooding poses severe risks to civil infrastructure, traffic flow, and public safety during monsoon seasons. The **Urban Flood Prediction & Pre-Monsoon Readiness System** integrates hydrological analysis, terrain elevation models, and rainfall data to identify waterlogging hotspots and recommend preventive drainage interventions before heavy downpours occur.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- 🌧️ **Pre-Monsoon Vulnerability Mapping**: Pinpoints high-risk urban inundation zones using elevation and drainage topology.
- 📊 **Runoff & Flow Modeling**: Estimates surface runoff volumes based on catchment characteristics and rainfall intensity.
- 🚨 **Early Readiness Indicators**: Provides actionable alerts for municipal authorities and disaster response teams.
- 🗺️ **GIS & Spatial Analysis Ready**: Compatible with spatial mapping layers and open geospatial datasets.
