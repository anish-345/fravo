# Fravo — Hackathon 2-Minute Demo Video Script

**Target Length**: 2 Minutes 15 Seconds  
**Format**: Screen recording of phone/emulator with voiceover or crisp background music + captions.

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] Hook: The Problem & The Core Concept
- **Visual**: Quick shot of someone scrolling Instagram/TikTok in bed with a screen time warning popping up and immediately being dismissed.
- **Voiceover**: 
  > *"Every day, students and young professionals lose 4 to 6 hours to doomscrolling. Traditional limiters don't work because you can bypass them in 2 taps. Meet Fravo — the app that turns screen time from a guilty habit into a healthy earned reward."*
- **Visual**: Open Fravo app. Show Pippy the cute animated mascot jumping on the dashboard.

---

### [0:25 - 0:55] Core Feature Walkthrough: Walk to Earn
- **Visual**: Show the Home Dashboard. Point out:
  - Steps today: `2,450`
  - Screen Time Bank: `35 mins remaining`
  - Blocked Apps: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"With Fravo, 1,000 steps equals 30 minutes of unlocked distraction time. Once your banked minutes run out, Fravo gently locks your selected distraction apps until you get up and walk."*
- **Visual**: Tap an app like Instagram. The sleek Fravo blocker screen appears with Pippy reminding you: *"Time is up! Walk 1,000 steps to unlock 30 mins."*
- **Visual**: Simulate walking / step count incrementing. Show the bank replenishing in real time!

---

### [0:55 - 1:25] OneSignal & AI Bandit Notification Engine
- **Visual**: Switch to a push notification arriving on the lock screen:
  - Title: *"Instagram is locked 🔒 (Tough Love)"*
  - Body: *"Screen time bank is empty. Take a quick stroll around the block to earn 30 mins!"*
  - Action buttons: `[Walk 5 Min]` `[Bank Steps]`
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"To keep users motivated, Fravo features an intelligent AI notification backend built in Rust. It syncs daily with OneSignal to understand each user's companion personality, primary distraction app, and streak risk. Using a Multi-Armed Bandit algorithm, it continuously learns which copy drives the highest walking conversions and optimizes retention automatically."*

---

### [1:25 - 1:55] RevenueCat Subscriptions & Judge Promo Code
- **Visual**: Tap the "Upgrade to Pro" card or Settings -> Subscription.
- **Visual**: Beautiful glassmorphic RevenueCat paywall appears:
  - Highlights: 2x Reward Rate (1k steps = 60 mins), Unlimited App Blocking, Zero Ads.
- **Voiceover**:
   > *"Fravo's monetization is powered by RevenueCat. Users can subscribe monthly, annually, or earn free 7-day Pro passes by entering an invite code through our viral referral loop. For judges, entering our invite code unlocks instant 7-day Pro access!"*
 - **Visual**: Type the invite code in the referral card -> Green confirmation toast appears -> Pro badge activates!

---

### [1:55 - 2:15] Closing & Call to Action
- **Visual**: Showcase Pippy's emotional companion screen with personality toggles (*Tough Love*, *Friendly*, *Stoic*), plus the Android Home Screen widget.
- **Voiceover**:
  > *"Fravo solves digital addiction not by taking your phone away, but by rewarding movement. Built with Flutter, RevenueCat, and OneSignal. Thank you for watching!"*
- **Visual**: End screen with GitHub repository links and Devpost submission info.
